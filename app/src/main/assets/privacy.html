Privacy Policy

VOTReader · last updated 5 October 2026

VOTReader is a reader for The Volumes of Truth and the Holy Bible, made by a disciple for personal study. This page says what the app does with information about you. In short: nothing you write leaves your device, and the app sends nothing about how you use it.

No accounts

There are no accounts, no sign-in and no passwords. The app does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details.

What you write stays on your device

Your notes, highlights, bookmarks, links, journal (with its photos and voice memos), reading progress, history and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. There is no server that holds them.

The phone app sends nothing

The Android app keeps no record of how you use it and sends no statistics, analytics or crash reports. There are no ads and no tracking.

The website counts visits

The website version at votreader.github.io/app counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics: one anonymous page view each time the site is opened. It uses no cookies and does not see anything you read or write. Only the maker sees the totals. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Recordings, songs and pictures

The reading text is inside the app. Recordings, songs and the pictures of A Return to The Garden are downloaded from the app's own release files on GitHub when you open them, so GitHub, like any web host, receives that request. A recording or song you save for offline listening is kept on your device. On the website, saving a recording for offline passes through the app's own relay, which keeps no logs.

Backups

A backup is a file you save yourself, wherever you choose. On Android, the app also keeps a weekly copy of your data in Downloads/VOTReader on your phone; you can turn it off in Settings › Your data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes what it stored on the device, except backup files you saved.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
