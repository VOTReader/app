  • The list always holds the Matthew Study Bible, so the three empty-list states (Loading, Nothing to list, Couldn’t load) do not appear today. While the letter studies download, the screen shows Matthew and the external site, with no loading line.
  • Some couldn’t load is the error a reader can meet. Try again restarts the download.

  • Recordings today: Matthew and Purity read along in full, Lamb of God in 14 of 16 parts; the other five have none.
