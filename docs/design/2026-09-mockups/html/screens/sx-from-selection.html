What travelsFrom the toolbar to the search box
The words, exactly. The whole selection becomes the query, punctuation and all: “again,” and “God.” are searched and marked with their comma and full stop.
Where you were. Opened from John 3, the chip offers “Search in John”; one tap keeps the same words to John.
No suggestions. Past 40 characters the suggestion list stays away, so the results start right under the chip.
Remembered when sent. Opening a result keeps the whole sentence as a recent search (unless History is off).
The toolbar row. Note, Link, Copy, Share, Search, Bookmark. Link and the colour row need a selection that can be highlighted; Listen from here appears only on text with a recording.
