Auto-Scroll
Adds a small play/pause pill to chapter and letter screens that scrolls the page for you at a steady reading pace. Touching the screen pauses it instantly; it picks back up a moment after you lift your finger. The pill fades out of the way while it runs.

How fast the page moves on its own. The ± buttons on the reading pill adjust this too, without leaving the page — and the pill shows your words per minute, measured from the page you are on.
Auto-Continue
When auto-scroll reaches the end of the text, count down and turn to the next page on its own, the way a swipe does: on into the next chapter, letter, book, volume or study. It stops at the very end, and after a long unattended run.
