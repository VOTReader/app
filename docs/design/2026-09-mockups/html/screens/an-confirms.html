DELETECancelDelete Everything
Proposed
  1. Ask about the thing, by name. “Remove this highlight?” “Delete this entry?”
  2. Say what else goes, in one plain line, and only when something does.
  3. Cancel on the left, quiet; the verb on the right, in one muted rose: Remove, Delete or Discard, the same word as the question. No “Yes,”.
  4. Where Cancel could be misread, over unsaved words, it says Keep editing.
  5. Weight is steps, never new words. One step for marks, links, bookmarks, notebooks and drafts; a second, named “Delete forever”, for your own writing; the typed word only for clearing everything.
Remove takes a mark or a link off the page; Delete erases something you made; Discard drops what was never saved. Journal entries take three steps today (Corbin’s call, July 28); rule 5 would make it two, so that one is his to make.
