Behaviour notesFrom ScripturesHome.jsx and index.html
  • Opening this screen starts the Bible and Matthew downloads, so a tap on a book rarely waits. A book tapped before the text arrives opens “Loading Bible…” at once.
  • Genre Grid: the Law, History, Poetry & Wisdom, Major and Minor Prophets; Gospels, Acts, Paul’s Epistles, General Epistles, Revelation. Acts and Revelation go straight to the book’s chapter list; the others open a genre page. In every layout a one-chapter book (Obadiah, Philemon, 2 John, 3 John, Jude) opens its chapter.
  • Compact List and Canonical Scroll carry an Old Testament and New Testament break; the Book Grid does not.
  • The translation line follows Settings › Bible Translation, e.g. “NKJV Restored Name” for NKJV-R.
