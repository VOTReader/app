How a stop is builtSame parts every time

Dim everything except the one thing to look at.

Ring it in thin gold, 8 px clear of its edge, in its own light.

Card beside it with a small pointer; on stops that show something on the text, the card docks to the bottom instead.

Row: Skip on the left, then Back and the one gold button.
