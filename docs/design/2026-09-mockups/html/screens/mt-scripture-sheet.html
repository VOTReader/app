Behaviour notesFrom ScriptureSheet.jsx, GoToRefButton.jsx and ChapterView.jsx
  • Only references whose verse text is in the app open the sheet. So its fallback line, “Verse text not available in app data”, never shows.
  • The text is always the NKJV, whichever Bible translation is chosen.
  • Open … closes the sheet and opens that chapter with the verses flashed. A “Back to Study Bible · Matthew 23” pill returns to the notes.
  • Close by tapping the grab bar or the dimmed page, or with Back or Escape. The app also puts a ‹ close at the top left; sheet.html leaves it out, and so do these.
  • On a screen 1640 px wide or more the sheet docks as a panel on the right, and the page stays live beside it.
