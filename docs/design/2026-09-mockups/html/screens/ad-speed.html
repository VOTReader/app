Speed on the desk opens one tool with four ways in: type a value, step by 1 %, slide, or tap a preset. Every way lands on a 1 % step from 0.5× to 3×.
Speed, opened from the deskThe readout is also a button; − and + move 1 % a press and repeat when held

Like a gemstone shining in the midst of all this darkness, easily found by the eyes of God, so shall you be in the day you surrender your life, the day you give up this fight and return to Me.
