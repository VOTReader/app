YahuShua Is Buried by Joseph of Arimatheia and Nicodemus

Now when evening had come, there came a rich man from Arimatheia, named Joseph, who himself had also become a disciple of YahuShua. This man went to Pilate and asked for the body of YahuShua. Then Pilate commanded the body to be given to him. When Joseph had taken the body, he wrapped it in a clean linen cloth, and laid it in his new tomb which he had hewn out of the rock; and he rolled a large stone against the door of the tomb, and departed. — Matthew 27:57-61

And they made His grave with the wicked—but with the rich
At His death, because He had done no violence,
Nor was any deceit in His mouth.
— Isaiah 53:9
