Volume One · Letter 15
Know That Which Has Been Poured Out, The Word of God
Thus says The Lord: How is it you have not recognized that which comes from The Father? For that which is of the devil is made plain and surrounds you on every side. Therefore the things of God become that much more precious, even as you, My son, are most precious in My sight... Like a gemstone shining in the midst of all this darkness, easily found by the eyes of God, so shall you be in the day you surrender your life, the day you give up this fight and return to Me. Do you not understand that I have saved you? No, not by your own works, but by My life, which I willingly gave up for you. Therefore, turn to Me wholly and in truth. And no more let the doctrines of men in the churches steal from your trust; nor let their traditions pollute your faith anymore. Be set apart from the ways of this world, and depart from the churches of men. For I alone am your faith, I alone am your completion.

How the chip movesFrom the chip at rest

A brief tap on a mark that is not a note opens the chip where you tapped: Color, Style, Note and Remove.

  • Color and Style swap the row in place; ‹ goes back.
  • Choosing a colour or a style applies it and closes the chip.
  • Remove never acts at once; it asks, naming the kind of mark.
  • A tap outside, or Back, closes it.

The toolbar’s ✕ asks too, and says so when a note would go with the mark.
