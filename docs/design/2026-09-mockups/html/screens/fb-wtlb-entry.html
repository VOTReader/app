Words To Live By · Part One · Entry 70
Walk Humbly with Your God

Hear and understand: The moment pride enters a man, and the moment he stands in front of Me to look elsewhere, is the same moment My hand is removed. For I have shown you, My servants, what is good and that which I require: That you should do justly, love mercy, and walk humbly with your God. Micah 6:8

Yet how can one behold My face while
Looking elsewhere, seeking glory for themselves?

And how can one who has stepped
In front of Me walk in My footsteps?

Beloved, how shall one who runs away from Me,
To tread another path, take My hand?...

Says The Lord.
