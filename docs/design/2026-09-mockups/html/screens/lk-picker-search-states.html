How the two scopes differFrom the source
Titles & refs is the default. It reads the app's own index of names, references and abbreviations, so “1 pet 1:23”, “V1 L15” and “repent” all land.

Full text reads the words inside every verse and letter. The corpus chips narrow it, and they appear only here. A chip is kept for this visit only; the Search screen's own choice is not changed.

A tap on a chapter opens the verse picker, a tap on a letter, entry or study chapter opens the excerpt picker, and an exact verse links at once.
