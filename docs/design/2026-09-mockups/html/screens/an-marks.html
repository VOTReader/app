Why these tenCalm, and still told apart
Today’s marks are highlighter ink (#FFD700, #76FF03, #FF4081 at 30-55% over black). These are the kit’s six calm tones plus four new ones, checked in both themes:
  • every pair of washes at least ΔE 6.5 apart (the kit’s own closest pair: 6.8)
  • text on any wash at 9.5:1 or better
  • every line and icon at 3:1 or better; Vellum deepens yellow, green and blue lines
  • closest pairs: pink and red, yellow and brown
