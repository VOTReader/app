This study presents the true chronology of The Messiah’s crucifixion and resurrection, tracing the events from the anointing at Bethany through His glorious rising on the Feast of Firstfruits. The standard traditional account of a Friday crucifixion and Sunday morning resurrection does not fulfill the sign of the prophet Jonah — three days and three nights in the heart of the earth. The true chronology honors both The Scriptures and The Sign.
