How the word is chosenOne rule for every library screen

Read-along when a recording ships and its words are timed. A study with some chapters recorded is still read-along; the count beside it keeps that honest.

Listening only when an edition declares its recording untimed. Painting words it cannot match would be worse than painting none.

No recording when nothing is recorded. The row still opens to read.

The count appears only for a partly recorded study, never when all or none of it is recorded.
