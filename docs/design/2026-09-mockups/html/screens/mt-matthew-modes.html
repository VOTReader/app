Behaviour notesFrom ChapterView.jsx, ModeToggle.jsx, StudyPanels.jsx, InlineNotes.jsx and InlineEcho.jsx
  • PDF (the default) keeps the verses clean and gathers everything after the last verse: Scripture References, Volumes of Truth Notes, then Further Study.
  • Inline hangs each note under the last verse of its first range (23:8-10 sits under verse 10). “↑” echoes mark the end of every later range and scroll back to the note.
  • A note opens its letter, with “Back to Matthew 23”. A scripture reference opens the scripture sheet (mt-scripture-sheet); a commentary line with no verse behind it is plain text with no chevron.
  • HM marks Hidden Manna. Only “Woe to Dallas” (23:32, 36) is in the app; this note is its only way in. Hidden Manna has no index, search result or Home tile.
  • The mode and whether notes show are kept per tab.
