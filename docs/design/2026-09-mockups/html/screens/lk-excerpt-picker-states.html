If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness. (1 John 1:9)

· What is true repentance? True repentance is two-fold: First, we must acknowledge our sin, and repent, asking The Lord for forgiveness. Second, we must strive toward purity, and endeavor to no longer sin. Both are required for true repentance.

Bear fruit in keeping with repentance. (Matthew 3:8)
