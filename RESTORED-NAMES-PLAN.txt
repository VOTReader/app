DOCTRINE BASIS (why these forms)
--------------------------------
The Volumes of Truth — "Death and Deliverance": "I command all who have ears to
hear, to call upon His name as it is, was, and has always been, and not as they
presume it to be… YAHUSHUA HAMASHIACH, both Lord and Savior of men." And
"Proclaim The Name of The Lord": "Speak My name as it is, and also in the
tongue of the hearer." (Also read: answersonlygodcangive.com/The_Name_of_The_Lord
and /Regarding_Sacred_Names.)
  - YahuShua = "YAH Is Salvation" (Zech 6:11 — the Hebrew of "Joshua" is
    Yahushua יהושע; Ps 118:14).
  - Ha = the definite article; Mashiach = "Anointed" (Messiah). HaMashiach =
    "The Messiah".
  - The commanded full Name is always "YahuShua HaMashiach" — the VOT corpus
    writes it 121x and NEVER in reversed order.

LANGUAGE ANALYSIS (how the ruleset was derived)
-----------------------------------------------
Every occurrence in both bases was cross-joined against the Textus Receptus
Greek (the base text behind the KJV and NKJV NT; Stephanus 1550/1894, public
domain, via getbible.net). Key findings that shaped the rules:

  - Greek Χριστός TRANSLATES Hebrew מָשִׁיחַ (mashiach, "anointed") — it is not
    a name transliteration. Restoring it yields Mashiach; with the Hebrew
    article, HaMashiach.
  - English "the Christ" is ALWAYS articular Greek (ho Christos) — 48/48 NKJV,
    18/18 KJV. Bare English "Christ" splits ~50/50 articular/anarthrous in the
    Greek, but Greek drops articles after prepositions as pure syntax — Hebrew
    puts the article on a definite title regardless (Delitzsch's Hebrew NT
    renders standalone Christos as HaMashiach in both cases). So ALL standalone
    Christ → HaMashiach, and the Greek article is NOT the splitting criterion.
  - Hebrew grammar forbids the article on a suffixed/construct noun: "His
    Christ" (Ps 2:2 meshichō, quoted Acts 4:26; Rev 11:15, 12:10) and "the
    Lord's Christ" (Luke 2:26, construct) → bare "Mashiach". Likewise the
    predicate in Acts 2:36 ("made Him… Lord and Mashiach") and "who is called
    Mashiach" after a naming verb (Matt 1:16, 27:17, 27:22).
  - Exactly ONE vocative exists (Matt 26:68 Χριστέ) → HaMashiach (Hebrew
    vocatives take the article; so Delitzsch).
  - The KJV itself reverses the TR's "Christ Jesus" to "Jesus Christ" in 11
    verses — pair order was never treated as inspired, which supports
    normalizing pairs to the commanded Name order.
  - Six verses print the Name in CAPITALS and my case-sensitive scan initially
    missed them — the naming verses (Matt 1:21, 1:25; Luke 1:31, 2:21 — where
    "you shall call His name YAHUSHUA, for He will save His people" only makes
    sense in Hebrew) and the cross inscriptions (Matt 27:37; John 19:19) →
    YAHUSHUA.

THE RULESET (tools/gen-restored-nt.mjs — regenerate there, never hand-edit)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Counts are (NKJV / KJV) instances. Ordered; first match wins.
  JESUS (caps)              → YAHUSHUA                    (6 / 6)

  Christ Jesus              → YahuShua HaMashiach         (69 / 58) [commanded order]
  false christs/Christs     → false messiahs/Messiahs     (Matt 24:24, Mk 13:22)
  the Christ (definite)     → HaMashiach ("the" absorbed — Ha IS the) (55 / 18)
  Christ's (bare)           → Mashiach's                  (14 / 14)
  Christ (every other)      → Mashiach  (name-like/anarthrous)      (244 / 281)
  Jesus' (possessive)       → YahuShua's                  (9 / 10)
  Jesus                     → YahuShua                    (710 / 708)
  ─ the former His-/Lord's-/called-Christ and Acts-2:36 "Lord and Christ" rules
    are GONE: each is just a bare "Christ" (no printed "the"), and the general
    bare rule now yields "His Mashiach", "the Lord's Mashiach", "called Mashiach",
    "both Lord and Mashiach" identically — one rule, no special cases. The John
    1:41/4:25 glosses and the Acts 7:45 / Heb 4:8 Jesus→Joshua fixes remain
    hand-adjudicated EXCEPTIONS (below).

PER-VERSE EXCEPTIONS (hand-adjudicated)
---------------------------------------
  Col 4:11    UNCHANGED — "Jesus who is called Justus", Paul's coworker. The
              Matthew Study Bible's own precedent governs all non-Lord bearers:
              "the names of other people were not corrected. Only The Messiah's
              name was restored."
  Acts 13:6   UNCHANGED — Bar-Jesus the sorcerer (protected token).
  Acts 11:26, 26:28, 1 Pet 4:16 — "Christian(s)" KEPT (VOT corpus uses it 53x).
  1 John 2:18/2:22/4:3, 2 John 7 — "antichrist(s)" KEPT (VOT uses it 16x).
  Acts 7:45 + Heb 4:8 (KJV only) — "Jesus" → "Joshua": the referent is Joshua
              son of Nun (Greek Iēsous covers both names); NKJV already reads
              Joshua, and a leftover "Jesus" would read as the Lord's retired
              name.
  John 1:41 + 4:25 — John's glosses translate Aramaic "Messias" into Greek for
              his readers ("which is translated, the Christ"). Restored:
              "We have found the Mashiach" (which is translated, the Anointed) —
              rendering the gloss by MEANING avoids "Mashiach…, that is,
              Mashiach" circularity.
  (Acts 2:36 "both Lord and Mashiach" is NO LONGER a coded exception — the
   general bare-Christ rule produces it. The 2026-07-20 re-audit removed it.)

ARCHITECTURE
------------
  - SPARSE OVERLAYS: bible-rnkjv.js / bible-rkjv.js carry ONLY changed verses
    (~180 KB each, vs 4.7 MB for a full copy). translateVerse falls through:
    rnkjv → verse.text (the NKJV base corpus); rkjv → BIBLE_KJV (registry
    `base:'kjv'`, a new field on TRANSLATION_OPTIONS) → verse.text.
    loadTranslation loads an overlay's base alongside it; the PERF-3 verse
    index is now a small LRU Map (chain lookups alternate two keys/chapter —
    a single-entry cache would rebuild per verse).
  - Registry: index.html TRANSLATION_OPTIONS ('rnkjv' after nkjv, 'rkjv' after
    kjv) — Settings selector, hero eyebrow, home-card tag, SE5/SCR1 allowlist
    all flow from it with no extra wiring.
  - SW: the corpus-cache regex (/src\/data\/bible-[a-z-]+\.js$/) auto-covers
    both files (cache-on-use); CORPUS_VERSION c12→c13 (+ CORPUS_CONTENT_VERSION
    in src/search/cache.js — gate-enforced equality).
  - Chrome (chapter titles/headings) is the separate, pre-existing
    settings.restoredNames layer (BOOKS_RESTORED, default ON) — restored chrome
    composes with restored verses on both new editions.
  - Validation: tools/validate-schemas.js Format E gained `sparse` entries —
    gap warnings suppressed (sparseness is the design), completeness swapped
    for an INVERSE subset check (every overlay verse must exist in BIBLE_KJV).
  - check_balance.py / esprima and the corpus-version lock cover the new files
    automatically (the DATA_CORPUS glob matches bible-r*.js).
